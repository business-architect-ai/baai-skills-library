# PRD Template

Use this template when creating the initial PRD from the user's brain dump (Phase 1). Keep under 500 lines total.

---

```markdown
**Created**: <date>
**Status**: Draft
**Source**: User brain dump

---

## 1. Vision

What is this? What problem does it solve? Who is it for?

<2-3 paragraphs synthesized from brain dump>

## 6. Explicit Constraints

- <Constraint>

## 7. Success Criteria

What does "done" look like? How will the user know the project succeeded?

- <Criterion>

```

---

## Guidelines for PRD Creation

- **Preserve the user's intent** - don't add features they didn't mention
- **Capture everything** - even vague signals belong in the PRD
- **Flag ambiguity** - put unclear items in Open Questions
- **Stay under 500 lines** - this is a synthesis, not a spec
- **Use the user's language** - don't over-formalize their ideas
- **Don't make technical decisions** - that happens in Discovery
